What can ACP do?

See your library

  • The sky map draws every field you have imaged, and clicking one opens its detail.
  • Targets on the rail lists your library with hours per filter, and its search box takes names or key:value terms.

Narrow the view

  • Filters on the rail chooses filters, telescopes, minimum hours and finished or unfinished targets.

Find new targets

  • The Sky menu in the top bar swaps the background survey, and More surveys searches every survey Aladin offers.
  • Library on the rail holds catalogue overlays, public survey footprints and the gap finder.

Plan

  • The Planning switch in the top bar opens your plans, where you frame a target, set mosaics and set filter goals.
  • Edit gear in the plan list holds your telescopes, cameras, filters and exposure templates.

Time and site

  • The Site menu in the top bar sets where you observe from.
  • Time-aware in the top bar adds visibility curves, a best tonight sort and horizon lines for that site.

Send to NINA

  • With the ACP NINA plugin, its dock in NINA's Imaging tab pushes plans to Framing or writes them into Target Scheduler.
  • Without the plugin, Manual Sync to NINA in the plan list exports your plans as a zip for Target Scheduler to import.
  • Live progress needs plugin 3.3 or later. The plugin reports captured frames to ACP, so plan hours update with no extra setup.

Set up outside the app

  • Your image folders and library scans are on the Settings page. Scans on a schedule run from the command line, see setting up your archive.
  • A friend's coverage loads as a layer through a setting. See sharing coverage.
  • The public page of what you are shooting is published by a script. See publishing a live page.

Keyboard and mouse

  • Right-click a field on the map, or a row in the list, for its menu.
  • Click the same spot again to step through fields that overlap.
  • Esc, or a click on empty sky, goes back one level in the panel.
  • With a plan open, drag its centre marker to move it or its corner handle to rotate it.
  • Press ? to open this panel.
